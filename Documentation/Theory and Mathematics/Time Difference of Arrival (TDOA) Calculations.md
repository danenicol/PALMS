# Time Difference of Arrival in a Three Dimensional Hydrophone Array for Multilateration
Detailed explanation of the equations used to calculate horizontal and vertical TDOA from the three-hydrophone array in freshwater conditions. Includes geometric relationships between pinger position, array spacing, and incident angle, as well as conversion of TDOA into spatial coordinates. Special focus is given to signal path differences for both horizontal and vertical baselines.

## Hydrophone Geometry && Unit-Vector Convention
3D right-handed coordinate system: 

The x-axis: horizontal, along the baseline of your two horizontal hydrophones (A → B). Positive x goes from A to B.

The y-axis: horizontal, perpendicular to the baseline, left/right when looking along +x.

The z-axis: vertical, pointing upward.

Horizontal pair: Hydrophone A and Hydrophone B, spaced horizontally by D (D = 2 ft = ~0.6096 m).

Vertical pair: Hydrophone B and Hydrophone C, with C placed vertically h above B (h = 1 ft = ~0.3048 m).

### Coordinate System: (x, y, z)
Hydrophone A: (-D/2, 0, 0) --> Left Hydrophone.

Hydrophone B: (+D/2, 0, 0) --> Right Hydrophone.

Hydrophone C: (+D, 0, h) --> Upper Hydrophone.

### Source Direction
Azimuth (𝜙): horizontal angle measured in the x-y plane, from the +x-axis (A --> B) toward +y.

Elevation (ε): vertical angle measured from the horizontal x–y plane upward along +z.
